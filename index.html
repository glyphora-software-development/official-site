How a project runs

Seven steps, each with something you can review.

  1. Requirements call

    We map your workflow, users and must-have features.

  2. Proposal and quote

    A written scope, milestones, timeline and fixed price.

  3. Prototype

    Clickable screens for you to approve before we write backend code.

  4. Build in iterations

    Working builds every one to two weeks, so feedback lands early.

  5. Testing and acceptance

    We test against the agreed scope, then you sign off.

  6. Deployment and training

    Go-live on your server or cloud, plus a walkthrough for your team.

  7. Support

    A bug-fix warranty period and optional maintenance after launch.
